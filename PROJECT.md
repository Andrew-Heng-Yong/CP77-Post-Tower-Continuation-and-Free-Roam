## Project name

**Working title:** Post-Tower Free Roam / Tower Continuation Mod

The project may be renamed later. The design target is a post-ending sandbox, not a new story campaign.

---

## Summary

This project adds a GTA-style free-roam mode after **The Tower** ending in *Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty*.

The Tower itself should play completely normally. After the final cutscene and credits, the mod returns directly to gameplay in the retained post-Tower session without loading a pre-ending save. It then restores only compatible sandbox content while preserving The Tower as completed and retaining as much canonical 2079 state as practical.

The former mandatory **Continue in Night City / Return to Main Menu** popup is no longer part of the v1 target.

The mod does **not** rewrite The Tower, reverse its consequences, create a happier ending, or attempt a complete new 2079 storyline.

---

## Design principle

> Preserve the ending first; restore gameplay second.

The desired state is:

```text
Tower completed
+
2079 / Tower world state
+
playable Night City
+
post-Tower V limitations
```

The player should regain access to the sandbox without reverting V to a pre-ending state.

---

## Motivation

Most base-game endings provide a One More Gig path that returns the player to Night City.

The Tower instead ends the playable timeline entirely.

That removes access to:

- exploration
- vehicles
- apartments
- vendors
- combat
- remaining open-world activities
- Dogtown
- the player's accumulated equipment and world progress

The intended experience is closer to GTA or Red Dead Redemption:

> The story is finished, but the sandbox remains playable.

The mod should preserve the consequences of The Tower rather than undo them.

---

## Canonical post-Tower state

### Must remain true

- The Tower is completed.
- V survived the Relic.
- Johnny is gone.
- The Relic storyline is finished.
- V spent roughly two years in a coma.
- relationship outcomes from The Tower remain intact.
- V cannot return to the same cyberware-heavy mercenary state.
- existing 2079-related state should remain where possible.
- `q307_2079` should ideally remain active.

### Must not happen

- Johnny suddenly returns.
- Nocturne OP55N1 becomes active again.
- Relic symptoms resume.
- romance states are blindly restored.
- V immediately reinstalls heavy combat cyberware and becomes pre-Tower V again.

## Technical premise

CET alone is not sufficient for the released implementation.

Testing already established that:

```text
Game.GetQuestsSystem():SetFactStr(...)
```

can change a quest fact without necessarily executing the quest-graph transition associated with that state.

Experimental fact manipulation caused saves to become stuck around:

- thug scene
- Misty scene
- intermediate q307 stages

Therefore:

1. do not manipulate q307 while The Tower is running
2. allow The Tower to complete naturally
3. activate the mod only after genuine q307 completion

## Definition of done for v1.0

v1.0 is complete when:

- The Tower finishes normally.
- credits play.
- the retained post-Tower session returns directly to gameplay after credits.
- no pre-ending/PONR save is loaded.
- V remains cured.
- Johnny remains gone.
- the selected No Nerf, Cyberware Only, or Full Nerf profile behaves consistently and persists.
- Tower state remains completed.
- as much existing 2079 state as practical is retained.
- normal sandbox systems work.
- selected non-story activities work.
- post-ending saves load reliably.

The final design objective is:

> The Tower still ends V's story, but it no longer ends the player's ability to inhabit Night City.
