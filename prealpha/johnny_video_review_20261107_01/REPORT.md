# Two-quest video review pilot

The user's latest workflow replaces transcript annotations with YouTube playthrough review and a removal list that can include spoken dialogue and conversation options. The pilot is **KOLD MIRAGE** and **Sweet Dreams**. Both already have source-qualified, registered post-Tower callers in the preserved five-quest review. Their source evidence is reused; no inventory rebuild or gameplay test was needed.

The editable notes form is `output_03/Two_quest_removal_notes.txt`. The user will put the quest title above each group and copy at least five consecutive words prefixed with the speaker, such as `Johnny: …` or `V: …`; `OPTION: …` identifies a conversation choice. A timestamp is optional unless it is needed to resolve multiple matches. For a line or option shorter than five words, the complete text is enough. Game IDs and the old three-before/five-V-after context quota are not required from the user. An explicit request such as “all Johnny dialogue in this quest” is accepted; template examples and blank fields do not select cuts. The user's five-word format example matched one Johnny event in the preserved KOLD MIRAGE source, solely as a format check; it was not imported as a removal request. The previous J5 documents and their exact mappings remain available as source evidence.

The two YouTube links were located through web search under the titles **Kold Mirage Full Mission - Cyberpunk 2077** and **Cyberpunk 2077 - Sweet Dreams Mission Walkthrough [4K 60FPS]**. Direct page fetches failed, so video content, complete branch coverage, game-version parity and dialogue-option coverage were not verified. They are viewing starting points, not game-source authority. The user can substitute another video and record its URL.

The current matching contract is `review_protocol.json`. On return, spoken events and menu choices are mapped separately to exact source IDs, providers/hashes and branch contexts. Removing an option does not silently select its spoken reply or downstream branch. “Both” or a whole-exchange request is recorded explicitly. Unseen branches and ambiguous wording remain review cases. Choice edits require a concrete plan preserving progression, earned outcomes, acknowledgements, rewards and exits.
