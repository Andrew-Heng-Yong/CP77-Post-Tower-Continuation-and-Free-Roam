## Capture and ownership

The native probe watches the original resolution phase's five registered waits at their exact path, node and class. It samples the one-shot game-time timer on the time worker. The existing direct Tower gate copies the duration and unchanged phone options into separate save-local fields before its ordinary acknowledgement. An already-ready or queued reply defers. Global snapshots never populate these fields.

The hook suppresses the selected timer's expiration during the cut and lets native quest teardown run. Selected cleared slots stay reserved through listener uninitialization, clock-worker confirmation and a later native notification pass. The replacement is admitted only after native phase detachment for a watched owner, or after a loaded post-cleanup session has no competing registration. This uses the existing S29 root registration. The new phase registers the same authored timer and listeners with new private phase ownership. The native scheduler supplies the actual timer ID and deadline, and the listeners wait for the timer worker to confirm the new record. Saves made in free roam use the stock timer serializer and callback reattachment path.

The reply-only child retains the original reply, expiry, cancel and response sequence. Its terminal output ends the phone child. The donor's vehicle, reward, prefab and quest cleanup operations are outside this phone phase. Saved post-Tower controls show the original choice group and first option remain Active even after the original registrations disappear. The handoff therefore checks the saved choice state against the live phone and does not reissue the opening choice.
