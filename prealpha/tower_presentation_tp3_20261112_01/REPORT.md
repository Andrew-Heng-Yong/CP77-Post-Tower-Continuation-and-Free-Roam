# Setup cover stall after The Tower

In the 20:01 Toronto playtest, the installed TP2 build completed The Tower, returned control to V at H10, and passed the job checks. It then reported 11 of 12 checks complete while the cover still said “Checking available jobs.” The first and last 11/12 samples were 20:05:25 and 20:08:22. The game shut down at 20:08:27. The stock hand attachment was confirmed, and neither the loading cleanup request nor representation activation was logged. [Captured observation](evidence_01/observation.json)

Source review places the wait in the newly added presentation step, after the required job and control checks. The old method could keep returning without updating the visible stage or imposing a deadline. The existing logs do not identify which presentation guard held it. The UI had reported a transition back to `inkGameState` after the arrival loading sequence. That transition does not establish the later loading-layer controller count or travel flags.

The successor changes one setup-cover script. After mandatory checks pass, the cover shows “Restoring player appearance” and gives optional refresh prerequisites up to four seconds. Loading cleanup runs only when its existing travel and controller guards allow it. A skipped cleanup lets the arm refresh proceed. Once representation activation is queued, the next return runs for the same attached player before the cover rechecks setup, unless a scene, vehicle, workspot or other control owner has taken over. The change preserves the original job checks and fact, inventory and save write paths.

All 148 registered REDscript sources compiled. Package readback verified 51 paths and the one-script delta. Fifteen deployment and recovery fixture actions passed, and the closed-game preflight verified the current install. TP3 is prepared for installation with separate authorization. The setup release, first-person arms and first fast travel still require a fresh playtest.
