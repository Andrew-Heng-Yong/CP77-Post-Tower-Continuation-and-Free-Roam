The exact-engine freezer readback confirms stored group/deferred-input and wake-dispatch mechanisms, not a complete pause API. Codeware's exposed `ExecuteNode` creates root context mode 1; it is not selective suspension or saved-owner restoration. Keeping a handler registered does not itself stop elapsed timers, outside-system events or actors. Calling reverse-engineered methods was not used as a substitute for that missing contract.

## Proposals evaluated

1. Prevent only bulk journal failure: both independent root retirement paths still remove the selected owners.
2. Skip only the cut in standalone `q307.quest`: the actual saved roots are base/EP1, with their own retirement paths.
3. Skip all relevant ancestor shutdowns and keep killed receipts true: callback suspension, truthful ownership and Johnny routing fail the required contract.
4. Skip those shutdowns and leave killed receipts false: existing continuation/admission assumptions require redesign, while suspension and Johnny routing remain unresolved.
5. Move native jobs under a private root: saved addresses change; this needs explicit native migration and support-lease handling.
6. Treat the native area freezer as a universal pause switch: stored deferred inputs alone do not establish remaining-time preservation, all callback suspension or preserved-owner reload safety.

These are source/data counterexamples and qualification results. They are not a simulation of the full engine, and no changed graph was installed. A prospectively preserved-owner gameplay trial remains unrun because no candidate met its prerequisites. The user's instruction allows choosing adapters when preservation does not prove reusable; that is the selected path.
