The recorded runs support SC3's repair of the old permanent-stop problem. They also establish that aiming and reload events reach native facts in the restored world. They do **not** establish restored reload speech, every hologram expression, or completion of Skippy's one-time quest. No new restoration defect is demonstrated by these logs.

This review uses a pinned cumulative CET capture ending at **19:58:31 on 28 October**, Toronto time, the earlier pre-ending observations, native graph reviews, and a fresh hash check of the installed Skippy script, combined archive and XL registration. The capture ends during a partial load; it is not a statement about the game's later state. Duplicate fact emissions in `gamelog.log` and `scripting.log` are counted once. Across all **87 SC3 status entries**, none records `Stop=1` or `Stopped=1`, and none records `fresh`, `resume` or `story` as true. Logging is sampled and emits only changed status strings; this is bounded evidence, not a trace of every engine transition. Startup all-zero samples are not automatically pre-ending saves, and claims on different loaded save branches must not be counted as duplicate owners within one run.

**Reload remains the unresolved behavior.** The pre-ending reload starts at 11:44:21 and 11:45:01 are 40 seconds apart. The later restored starts at 13:59:15 and 13:59:26 are only 11 seconds apart. Native comments node103 starts a 30-second realtime rearm, separately from a 2.5-second reaction delay. If the first event reached that listener and the child stayed active, the second occurred before rearm; it is not an independent second speech opportunity. The status log shows no intervening `Active=0`, but it does not record listener execution or timer completion.

After the delay, the native route checks equipped Skippy, Tier1 and 19 protected reaction sections, then uses a two-output `IgnoreLastUsed` selector with only one output connected. Its admitted branch chooses `OnReload` or the emo refusal. A fact pulse clearing before 2.5 seconds does not cancel the already-triggered delay. The selector is not an independent 50% coin flip on every reload.

Both later restored reload starts occur while the recorded dialogue-interruption flag is high. Native comments child98 does set that flag and dispatch `skippy_scene_stop`; interruption is a real mechanism. However, the pre-ending reference also has high interruption flags, and a high level does not identify which reaction was selected or stopped. The logs lack selector outputs and active-section receipts. They therefore cannot distinguish an intentional silent choice, a gate failure, scene competition or an interrupted line for a particular reload. `holo_interrupt_call` is not proof that a particular Skippy hologram rendered.

The jump-confirm line has no authored jump listener or confirmation continuation. The user's pre-ending confirmation included aim/draw/reload/hologram, not jump acknowledgement.

This explains one unmet condition in that historical snapshot, without proving the current save's condition or diagnosing a new story failure.
