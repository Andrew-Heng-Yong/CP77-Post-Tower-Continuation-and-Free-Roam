Exact progress preservation remains required for each supported state. Existing reviewed stage-start fallbacks keep their separate rules. “Most stages” is a development target. Current evidence does not establish majority coverage. The source packet covers 160 selected quests and 1,849 phase/objective records, but those records are not a count of independently resumable execution states. A phase can contain multiple branches, concurrent listeners and different pending effects. Report qualified stages, exclusions and unknowns against a defined stage inventory before making a numerical coverage claim. [Source contracts](../prealpha/quest_resume_contracts_rc2_20261114_01/REPORT.md)

The most useful distinction is whether the quest is waiting at a stable boundary or is partway through an action. A stable boundary has identifiable pending listeners, settled earlier effects and recoverable world support. An objective such as “Meet someone” can still depend on an interrupted call or an unfinished actor command, so its displayed wording alone cannot establish difficulty or support.

The following ratings are engineering assessments from the reviewed source and bounded observations. They are priorities for qualification, not new runtime admission rules or claims of gameplay success.

| State at the interruption | Relative difficulty | Reason and proposed treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting for a fact, inventory item or journal event after earlier work has settled | Lower | Usually a small set of listeners and receipts. Prioritize these when the original owner, support and pending effects are known. The Gig's earned Cassius tattoo objective is a concrete structural example. |
| Travelling to a location or waiting to talk before a scene starts | Lower to moderate | The trigger, NPC and interaction must still exist under the correct owner. Support a settled approach stage separately from a conversation already playing. Kirk's route demonstrates that even this boundary can need a content-gate repair. |
| Waiting on a simple timer | Moderate | Preserve the applicable clock, timer registration, expiry race and successor. A small timer record can lead into a much harder stage. Natural expiry is acceptable when the actual successor is preserved; it must not restart the wait or fake downstream completion. |
| Several stable objectives or partial counters in parallel | Moderate to high | Preserve every pending branch and already-satisfied input, plus separate final rewards. Epistrophy's partial join is a useful fixture. The saved mask alone cannot restore the seven cab controllers and phone support. |
| Waiting for an initial text, or an already-delivered text awaiting a non-expiring read/reply | Moderate to high | Message state, issuing owner, read/reply listener, prior delivery and downstream gates must agree. A self-contained persistent message may be supportable. Message-related stages are not excluded as a class. |
| Reply choices with expiry, competing triggers or a follow-up owned elsewhere | High | Reply, timeout and visit branches can run concurrently and retire one another. Preserve choices, receipts and independent follow-ups together. Keep the existing scoped optional-reply exclusions and deprioritize similarly expensive cases. |
| A call, dialogue, cutscene or scripted interaction already playing | Very high | Playback position, actors, cameras, workspots, choice commitment and start/end acknowledgements can be transient. Exact interruption inside unqualified playback is outside the initial target. Stages before or after it remain candidates. |
| A fight, chase, race or coordinated vehicle/actor sequence already executing | Very high | Health, deaths, counters, actor roles, queued commands, vehicle state and failure paths must agree. Exact mid-action restoration without a qualified native state transfer is outside the initial target. A settled pre-encounter or post-encounter boundary is assessed separately. |
| Equipment held in escrow, or an item/reward transfer with an unresolved commit | Highest save risk | An incorrect replay can lose items or duplicate a payment. Leave these intervals unsupported until their transaction and recovery state are independently qualified. Sweet Dreams' confiscation-to-recovery interval is already excluded. A settled earned reward waiting for collection can be substantially easier. |

**Over the Edge is a concrete warning about text leads.** In the v0.1.10 MS31 run, the native wait expired, the handoff committed once and AutoSave-17 contained Falco's pending condition and Reyes's listeners. The player still received no message, and the saved quest and wait objective were Failed. The condition requires both the native SideQuest_MainPath token and being outside the Reyes-place trigger. The exact failing operand or callback path remains unknown. This proves that restoring registrations is insufficient to claim playable continuation. It does not prove that text messages themselves are impossible to resume, or that the timer should be reset. Keep this stage unqualified. Any further focused diagnosis can start from the preserved AutoSave-17 evidence; a new complete handoff qualification is a separate test. The revised implementation order is:

1. Qualify one contained stable stage end to end using the shared handoff, including actual objective advancement, one post-resume save/reload and no repeated effects. Preserve existing successful adapter fixtures without demanding repeat tests.
2. Expand reusable fact, inventory, journal and location waits with reviewed support. Qualify simple timers and then concurrent stable branches. Each class must account for its successor and cleanup, not just creation of its handler.
3. Admit message stages only when delivery/read/reply state and all gates can be restored coherently. Complex optional text tails and unqualified mid-playback, mid-encounter and transfer intervals do not block delivery of the supported feature. No new bespoke one-stage adapter programme is introduced by this scope change.
4. Maintain per-stage decisions: qualified exact continuation, existing reviewed fallback, intentionally unsupported, or unknown pending investigation. A recognized unsupported state defers. Missing evidence also defers but is not evidence that the stage is inherently difficult. An exception records the affected execution interval, cooperating owners, blocker, preserved history and any already-reviewed fallback.
5. Validate a mixed-progress selection drawn from the supported classes, including a base-game and EP1 case and inactive/terminal controls. Every selected supported state must advance and survive save/reload. Separately verify that unsupported samples do not start replacement owners or replay effects.

The original four whole-quest and five stage/branch exceptions remain in force. The new category-level exclusions guide the development target; they still need source-bound stage classification before a generalized dispatcher can enforce them. Existing qualified paths retain their bounded support. Unknown or excluded states follow normal Tower cleanup and may remain unavailable after the ending. No automatic rewind, forced completion, timer reset, reward replay or invented fallback follows from an exclusion. The game's genuine pre-Tower completed/failed history and all earned progress remain protected.

This review changes project scope and documentation. The installed v0.1.10 build and its recorded test result are unchanged. Broad stage coverage, Falco's message delivery and the shared feature's mixed-progress acceptance remain unproved.
