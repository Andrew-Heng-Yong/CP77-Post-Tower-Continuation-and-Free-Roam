## Native entry and preservation

- Love Rollercoaster copies the original `mq006` story root, Jean/party introduction, repair, ride, original journal/rewards, and the original minor-quest parent's q001/q005/q101 gates. Its wrapper claims `PTFRWORLD_Coaster_QuestOwns` before the story and releases it only after the native root returns. A fresh story requires the old repeatable coaster controller to be unregistered and its repeatable gate still off. Previously finished, issued, or partially progressed story state is protected.
- Shape of a Pony copies its 141-node native owner and two scenes, including El Capitán message, investigation, car encounter, and original Porsche reward. The adapter denies an already owned Porsche by both native ownership fact and vehicle-system check.
- Career Opportunities copies its 77-node native owner and scene, including the manufacturer messages and original reward. It requires the earned `shooter_appeared_on_leaderboard` fact before the entry can issue; a claimed reward or existing progress is protected.
- The new script class sits inside the existing S29 module/file. It does not alter the prior S29 class fields, source text, or its 16 existing entries. Each new job uses a read-only settled journal/fact/vehicle sample on two successive ticks, then grants only its own `_Permit` fact. Original quest state and rewards remain native. An issued entry is never restarted on load.
