# Project invariants and specialized policies

These refine root AGENTS.md. They are policy, not permission to change runtime state.

## Ending and presentation

The Tower completes normally, including its cleanup and credits. Continuation retains the post-ending session; no PONR/pre-ending reload. V stays cured, Tower relationships and 2079 state remain, and Nocturne/Relic/Johnny's engram do not resume. A minimal, evidenced official-resource patch is allowed; wholesale ending replacement is not the default.

R4 permits reviewed supporting Johnny voice and native timing/internal bindings while excluding his visible projection, takeover and Relic-dependent central story. The approved cast selection also excludes live-participant Judy, Panam, Kerry and Misty routes as recorded in the whitelist, including optional calls/messages; mentions and historical recordings are different. Consult [the final R6/R6.1 record](../whitelist_final_r6.md) and [catalogue](../whitelist_final_r6_catalogue.md), not title names alone. Scope exceptions such as preserving Kerry's earned house access do not authorize his excluded quests or conversations.

## Eligibility, ownership and save continuity

- Completed stays completed. Preserve genuine failures, earned choices, items, rewards, fixer/achievement counts, apartment receipts and snapshot provenance.
- The 22 October unfinished-content decision supersedes older fresh-only and “restart once” language: continue supported existing progress; defer unsupported stages. Never interpret that decision as a generic replay, reset or failure-to-fresh conversion.
- Excluded prerequisites require genuine pre-Tower completion with the appropriate outcome. Unknown history remains unknown. Preserve actual native AND/OR dependency logic. An included prerequisite can unlock a child through its own legitimate completion.
- Entry/title selection, journal presence, resource availability, community presence and executing ownership are separate facts. Neither a killed flag nor an absent mod receipt proves a native/saved owner is dead.
- Review the original owner, shared leases and exit/cut/cleanup paths before adding another. Keep in-flight start/end requests and acknowledgements. Do not hold an issued call behind an interruption flag that its own completion must clear.
- Preserve saved resource paths, node IDs, class/persistent-field identities and owned claims. A successor keeps prior resources byte-identical unless a reviewed change is listed. Model already-issued owners, interruptions, reloads, completed/failed histories and reward uniqueness.
- Save compatibility with future stable successors is a release requirement. Static checks and file rollback do not prove future save upgrades or reverse saved gameplay.

## Profiles and housing

The installed selector is [`profiles/active.json`](../../profiles/active.json), pointing to `tower_implant_focused/profile.json`. The implementation and migration rules are in [`profiles/README.md`](../../profiles/README.md). Historical No Nerf/Cyberware Only/Full Nerf proposals are not proof of an installed three-way selector. Preserve the actual active profile and its persistent revision.

The recorded active rules include Body 3, maximum HP 150, unchanged direct stamina, closed Relic, stock Basic Kiroshi/Casius permissions with functional smart-targeting denial, and the reviewed perk dependency catalogue. Do not generalize those rules into destruction of owned items or refunds of unknown modded perks. A no-vitals path must remain free of HP/stamina writes. Proposed balance changes require a separate decision and migration review.

Ordinary housing entitlement is lost until reacquired; geometry and shared stash contents are preserved. H10 repurchase is one €$25,000 transaction (a mod rule, not a vanilla sale price). Other ordinary homes use their normal prices without the obsolete 1.5× surcharge. Never-owned homes retain normal eligibility. Home-only services follow current entitlement. Relationship homes are not generic purchasable real estate. Kerry's existing earned house access is the explicit retained-access exception; do not fabricate access, romance or quest completion. See [H1](../housing_h1_playtest.md), [H5](../h10_h5.md), [H6/HS2](../h10_h6_snapshot_reload_hs2.md) and the archived ROADMAP housing section for provenance.

## World and resource integrity

Prefer native patterns and private support owners. Include root and nested prefab obligations, communities, workspots, spline/marker bindings, variants, scene actors/cameras and cleanup. Do not restart scripted fights, loot/progression controllers or full encounters merely to animate an idle actor. WA3's excluded progress-gated families and unreadable group1013 remain bounded exclusions, not unfinished permission to restore their unfiltered originals.

Do not confuse a working component sample with a whole family/game pass. The latest PH3 user report is valid for the reported phone fixture; 825 unique injection IDs, 997 examined quest phases and other historical counts describe those exact inventories only.

## Testing, release and authorization

Do not launch/close the game, modify saves/facts, install, clean obsolete files, publish or push unless authorized for that action. Use disposable test saves; keep master fixtures intact. Do not request repetitions of already-confirmed fixtures without a new concrete need or ask for more gameplay simply to expand a bounded success report.

Release qualification includes watched/skipped credits, genuine cleanup and retained session, control/map/vehicles/saving/reload, Tower/Johnny/Relic/profile invariants, enabled Dogtown and restored activity lifecycle/reward/save behavior. Record untested items explicitly. These are release gates, not a command to run them during read-only or documentation work.
