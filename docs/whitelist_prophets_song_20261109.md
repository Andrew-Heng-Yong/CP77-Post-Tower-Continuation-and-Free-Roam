# The Prophet’s Song post-Tower exclusion

On 9 November 2026 the user asked to remove The Prophet’s Song (`mq026_conspiracy`) from the post-Tower whitelist after reporting a scene transition that prevented them from playing it. R6.3 removes the job from both selection variants. The broader selection has 80 gigs and 80 side jobs. The conservative selection has 34 gigs and 28 side jobs. [Current selection](../research/gig_restoration/lore_whitelist_current.json)

JB2 closes new admission through both PTFR adapters. S23’s bounded continuation and S25’s fresh route withhold permission before issuing an owner. Each phase also blocks its issuance edge so a saved permission cannot start the quest while scripts initialize. Issued owners keep their registered phase paths, progression and cleanup.
