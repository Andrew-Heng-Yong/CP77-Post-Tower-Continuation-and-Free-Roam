26 October 2026. Read-only selection audit. The current R6.1 registry still selects 78 side jobs and 80 gigs. Its other decisions are 51 excluded records (50 side/minor records and one gig) and 18 separate-scope records. This pass re-screened all 69 decision rows, then inspected shipped quest resources for the cases most likely to hide a playable side job behind a world-service or reward label. It did not alter the whitelist, installed mod, game, saves, or facts.

## Selection test

Johnny appearing in a scene does not by itself make him the subject of the job. The operative question is whether V's task and outcome depend on Johnny's personal story, agenda, takeover, or Relic. Supporting lines can use the existing voice-only presentation rule, but a full quest still needs native entry, cast/props, prerequisites, cleanup, and once-only reward handling. Other established exclusions (live Judy/Panam/Kerry/Misty participation, impossible cured-V premise, profile requirements, fixed historical event, already completed progress) remain separate tests. No source finding below authorizes starting a quest over a saved owner.
